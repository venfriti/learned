Try Learned

This is the working prototype with a sample lesson on computer network protocols.

  1. Tap Resume to open the deck. It turns to landscape.
  2. On the DNS slide, tap I don’t get this.
  3. Tap Go deeper, then Keep for review.
  4. Use the arrows to finish the deck, then take the quiz.
  5. Open the Review tab. It shows a sample card.
Open the app Open in its own tab

Progress and settings stay in your browser. On a phone, open the app and use Add to Home Screen to install it.
